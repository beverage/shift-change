v1.3.1 update:

New setting, Keep colonists decent, in the mod settings. It is OFF by default, so nothing changes in a colony you are already playing unless you switch it on.

Turn it on and a stand will not leave a colonist without basic clothing: if what it hands out does not cover them, their innermost garments stay on instead, and the rest of the swap happens as normal.

It matters most with Change the whole outfit ticked, where a stand holding only a hat, a belt or a pair of trousers would otherwise send someone out with nothing else on.

Nudists, and anyone whose ideoligion prefers nudity, are never affected either way.
