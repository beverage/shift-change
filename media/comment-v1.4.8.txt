v1.4.8 update:

With Outfit Stands Plus installed, having a colonist selected costs less on a large colony.

That mod gives every colonist a button for their outfit stand, and to find which stands a colonist owns, it went through every building you had built, every frame the colonist stayed selected. On a colony of about 9,000 buildings that came to 0.8 ms per selected colonist per frame. It now goes through the outfit stands only.

The button works as before, with the same stands in the same order. If Outfit Stands Plus ever changes how it finds them, Shift Change leaves the button alone.

Without Outfit Stands Plus, nothing changes.
