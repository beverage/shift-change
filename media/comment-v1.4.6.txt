v1.4.6 update:

Ticking "Resting in bed" on a stand never dressed anyone. Both patient rows in the work list have been inert since sleepwear arrived, so a hospital stand set up the way the description says did nothing at all, and nothing on screen said so. Reported by a player who had it configured correctly.

It works now. Put a stand in the hospital, tick the row, stock it with gowns, and a colonist going to bed to recuperate changes there on the way.

A colonist who still needs a doctor goes straight to bed instead. Someone wounded in a raid gets the gown on their next trip, once they have been tended.

There is a new setting for the other preference, off by default: "Medical emergencies change first". Turn it on and a doctor answering an emergency tend puts scrubs on first, and a critical patient stops for the gown. Fires are never covered by it.
